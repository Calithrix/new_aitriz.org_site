Welcome to the Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies

TRIZ (pronounced TREEZ) is the Russian acronym for the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving. TRIZ is a systematic process that develops critical thinking skills and promotes creativity and innovation.

What is TRIZ

This proven algorithmic approach to solving technical problems began in 1946 when Genrikh Altshuller studied thousands of patents and noticed certain patterns.
