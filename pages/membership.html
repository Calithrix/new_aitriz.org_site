Membership

Membership in Altshuller Institute (AI) is important for you to grow your knowledge of TRIZ and to have access to a larger body of TRIZ providers and TRIZ users. Membership provides an array of benefits and allows our TRIZ community to grow and become stronger. Your participation as a member is the first step to becoming an effective influence in the growth and development of AI.

The Altshuller Institute is dedicated to fostering international collaboration in the field of TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving). When you become a member, you join a global network that builds partnerships with TRIZ organizations, professional societies, and research institutions worldwide; facilitates the exchange of ideas, case studies, and best practices; supports joint research projects and cooperative innovation efforts; and promotes the standardization of TRIZ practices.

Membership plans

Plans and prices as shown on the live registration page, Join us!:

  • Annual — USD 120.00 per year. Access to view the Institute's restricted archives of TRIZ materials (papers in past TRIZCON Proceedings, articles in Izobretenia, past Inside TRIZ and TRIZ Features articles, the Soft Side of TRIZ series of TRIZ lessons by Jack Hipple, reviews of past events from around the world), participating on Board committees as a volunteer, 10% discount on all items in the general store, 10% discount on the annual conference, plus all privileges granted to the free membership.
  • Student — USD 35.00 for 1 year. Only available to full-time students; all annual membership privileges. Subscribers must provide proof of full-time enrollment in an educational institution with an enrollment verification letter from that institution.
  • Retired — USD 60.00 for 1 year. Only available to retirees; all annual membership privileges. Subscribers must provide proof of retirement.
  • Lifetime — USD 950.00, one-time fee. A non-expiring membership with all annual membership privileges; you never have to renew your membership.
  • Free — for 6 months, renewable. Access to community pages; ability to submit items (news, web links, TRIZCON abstracts/workshops, other TRIZ related training or conference information); receive the AI Newsletter or Special Announcements each month. It does NOT give you access to view restricted materials reserved for paid annual members.

Corporate / group plans

Group membership allows corporations to provide numerous AI memberships and benefits to their workforce. Continued exposure to TRIZ and allied activities will solidify a support structure for TRIZ in their workplace. This is an annual membership and carries all the benefits of annual membership. Levels on the live site: Platinum $20,000 (500 Annual Individual Memberships, display space at TRIZCON), Gold $15,000 (300 memberships), Silver $10,000 (200 memberships), Bronze $5,000 (100 memberships) and Iron $2,500 (50 memberships), each with recognition on the Institute website. To purchase a group membership, contact the Altshuller Institute. Corporate/Group Plans

Join

Registration and checkout stay on the live member system: aitriz.org/membership/join. The Free membership to the Altshuller Institute is for six months and may be renewed. You may upgrade to any of the paid memberships at any time by logging into the website and editing your membership status.

Lifetime Members

The "Lifetime" members are the valued supporters of the Altshuller Institute. They have a vision that TRIZ will be a sustaining force in the improvements in products and processes in all industries and sciences. TRIZ will ultimately improve the lives of each individual worldwide. I encourage you to join this group of valiant and forward thinking leaders.

Donate & Special Projects

Please help to support the Altshuller Institute with a generous donation.

General support

It costs a lot of money to staff and maintain an international organization. Program development, building fund, IT support, advertising and promotion are only a few of the many expenses that need to be paid each year or we severely limit our capacity to grow and deliver viable programs. Donations to this program will be used to provide general support to AI for overhead, rent, utilities, wages and equipment. GOAL: A $500,000 endowment would insure that annual needs and stability of this Institute can be met. Your generous support of $100 or more would be a great start. Donations / Special Projects
