Why join

Influence, connection, and a chance to shape the future of TRIZ

The Altshuller Institute is dedicated to fostering international collaboration in the field of TRIZ. We are the only organization officially authorized by Genrikh Altshuller, the founder of TRIZ, to use his name. When you become a member, you join a global network that:

  • Builds partnerships with TRIZ organizations, professional societies, and research institutions worldwide.
  • Facilitates the exchange of ideas, case studies, and best practices.
  • Supports joint research projects and cooperative innovation efforts.
  • Promotes the standardization of TRIZ practices, ensuring a unified, effective approach to inventive problem solving.

Paid membership includes

  • Access to the Institute's restricted archives of TRIZ materials
  • Papers in past TRIZCON proceedings
  • Articles in Izobretenia
  • Past Inside TRIZ and TRIZ Features articles
  • Soft Side of TRIZ series of lessons by Jack Hipple
  • Reviews of past events from around the world
  • Participation on Board committees as a volunteer
  • 10% discount on all items in the General Store
  • 10% discount on the annual conference
  • Eligibility for Institute certification
  • Plus all privileges of the free membership

Lifetime members

Valued supporters with a vision

Our Lifetime members are the valued supporters of the Altshuller Institute. They have a vision that TRIZ will be a sustaining force in the improvement of products and processes in all industries and sciences — and that TRIZ will ultimately improve the lives of individuals worldwide.

The roll begins in 1998 with Genrikh Altshuller himself and Lev Shulyak, and has grown to include practitioners, consultants and academics from the United States, Korea, Japan, Europe and beyond. We encourage you to join this group of forward-thinking leaders.

Sponsorship & donations

Help us bring TRIZ to the world

The Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies is actively seeking financial sponsors to sustain and expand our year-round programs, events, and global outreach. Our 501(c)(3) status ensures complete deductibility of your personal contribution.

Legacy Support

Monthly or annual contributions designated to the General Fund. Every level includes an annual membership and recognition on the Institute website; higher levels add logo placement, TRIZCON passes and a Certificate of Appreciation presented at TRIZCON.

  • Level 1 — $500/month ($6,000/yr): logo on E-Newsletter and homepage, two TRIZCON passes, two memberships
  • Level 2 — $250/month ($3,000/yr): homepage logo, one TRIZCON pass, one membership
  • Level 3 — $100/month ($1,200/yr): homepage logo, one membership
  • Preferred — $50/month ($600/yr)
  • Honored — $25/month ($300/yr)
Sponsorship details

Website & newsletter sponsorship

Universal Website Sponsors have their logos and links placed prominently on the Institute website: $500 per year, $135 per quarter or $50 per month. Newsletter sponsors appear in the header of E-Newsletters and News Flashes sent to over 1,500 subscribers: $150 per quarter or $500 per year.

General support & conferences

Program development, IT support, advertising and promotion are only a few of the expenses that must be met each year. A $500,000 endowment would ensure the annual needs and stability of the Institute; a gift of $100 or more is a great start. Hosting or sponsoring a TRIZCON normally ranges from $10,000 to $50,000 depending on scope.
