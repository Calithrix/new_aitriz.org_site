About the Institute

The Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies is a 501(c)(3) non-profit that promotes the use of TRIZ in the world through conferences, certification, webinars, books and videos. TRIZ is the tool, you are the force, and the product is a better world.

History

Founded with Altshuller's blessing

In 1995, the team at Technical Innovation Center approached Genrikh Altshuller about creating a not-for-profit institution to promote TRIZ outside of Russia. Genrikh Altshuller gave his blessing for the idea and members of the TRIZ community went about realizing this initiative. In the fall of 1998, with the enthusiastic support of TRIZ users around the world, the Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies was established.

The Institute has continued to develop, serving the needs of a growing TRIZ community. We are the only organization officially authorized by Genrikh Altshuller, the founder of TRIZ, to use his name. Certification of TRIZ was enacted by Altshuller at the Institute's founding.

Today the Institute is a global organization that has developed a viable TRIZ certification process and promotes the universality of TRIZ in the world marketplace. We promote the use of TRIZ through our annual conference, TRIZCON, other global TRIZ events, webinars, books and videos, and we are developing TRIZ programs for STEM education.

Mission

Grow innovation and productivity with TRIZ methods

The Altshuller Institute believes that all men and women seek to be more creative and innovative. We believe that with the right tools and some training, people can become 10–30% more innovative and productive. TRIZ energizes and expedites the process of innovation; it is a natural amplifier of our talents, knowledge and experience.

Application of TRIZ methods to medicine, agriculture, manufacturing and business management can spur innovation and improve the human condition. Join us in making this world a better place.

Who we serve

  • Anyone looking for innovation, effective thinking skills, and problem solving techniques
  • Those who have heard about TRIZ and want to learn more
  • Businesses seeking to develop or expand their R&D efforts more productively
  • Schools interested in STEM — TRIZ is a perfect strand for systematic problem solving
  • Consultants and practitioners seeking tools to complement Six Sigma, Lean, QFD or other quality programs
  • TRIZ practitioners who wish to expand TRIZ to the world

Objectives

The primary objective of the Institute is the advancement of the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving and the allied arts and sciences, and maintenance of high professional standing among its members. The Institute's purposes are:

  • To create, promote, and stimulate interest in the advancement and diffusion of knowledge of the art and science of TRIZ and its application.
  • To promote unity, effectiveness of effort, and ethical professional conduct among those devoting themselves to the theory and practice of TRIZ.
  • To provide for conferences, conventions, and other meetings of its members for the exchange of ideas and experiences.
  • To publish and distribute books, pamphlets, periodicals and other printed matter devoted to TRIZ.
  • To preserve, maintain and make accessible documents relevant to the past, present and future development of TRIZ.
  • To make reliable information concerning TRIZ available to journals, newspapers and other channels of public information.
  • To honor Genrikh Altshuller and gratefully recognize his contributions as the scientific foundation for inventive problem solving.
  • To facilitate TRIZ research and development, translate TRIZ materials, develop and export TRIZ materials for children, and develop a certification program that standardizes proficiency levels.

Notes from the Executive Director

My feelings and observations

Welcome to the Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies.

After nearly 30 years of existence, the Institute came to recognize that it had fallen into its own form of "Paradigm Paralysis." While many of our past approaches served us well for over two decades, they were no longer effectively reaching or engaging today's audiences. Following several years of reduced activity, we realized it was time not simply to preserve TRIZ, but to reinvent how we communicate, teach, and apply it in the modern world.

Today, we are re-energizing the Institute with a renewed mission — not only to promote TRIZ, but to help people successfully apply TRIZ to real-world challenges, business growth, education, innovation, and personal development. We want individuals and organizations to experience the true power of systematic innovation and gain measurable benefits from its application.

Our goal is to build a strong global community of TRIZ practitioners, educators, researchers, students, and business leaders who believe in the value of structured creativity and advanced problem-solving. By helping people achieve greater success through innovation, we believe TRIZ can once again become a major force for progress in industry, education, and society.

We hope you will join us as we begin this exciting new chapter and help shape a future filled with greater creativity, innovation, success, and prosperity. Let's make Altshuller proud on his 100th anniversary!

Richard LangevinExecutive Director, The Altshuller Institute for TRIZ Studies
