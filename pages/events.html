Events

TRIZCON is an annual International event held by Altshuller Institute which provides an "OPEN" platform for users, providers, supporters, students to come together and exchange information. Each year a vast collage of professionals from all walks of life get to meet each other and exchange stories — making new friends. Come and join us each year and "Unchain your Brain."

Training courses from various providers are listed on the Training Calendar under Learn.

Awards

Every year at TRIZCON, members of the Altshuller Institute are recognized and honored for their contributions to the institute, or their outstanding work in other fields.

  • AI TRIZ Hall of Fame Award — the highest award given by the Altshuller Institute; it enters the awardee into the TRIZ Hall of Fame and recognizes a sustained high level of contribution to the practice and advancement of TRIZ principles. Winners: 2019 Dr. Pesakh Amnuel · 2018 Voluslav V. Mitrofanov · 2017 Alexander Selyutski · 2016 Genrick Altshuller.
  • AI Distinguished Service Award — the highest distinction for service to the Altshuller Institute and the missions for which it stands. Winners: 2019 Isak Bukhman · 2018 Donald Coates · 2017 Richard Langevin · 2016 Lev Shulyak.
  • AI Best Practice Award — presented to the company that has demonstrated the most outstanding leadership and significant results in the application of modern TRIZ methods. Winners: 2019 Dr. Boris Farber · 2018 James Bradley · 2017 Jack Hipple · 2016 Ideation International Inc.
  • AI Educational Excellence Award — for outstanding leadership in the practice of teaching TRIZ, especially as an academic subject or theoretical concept. Winners: 2019 Prof. Kartik Ariyur · 2018 Denis Cavallucci · 2017 Victor Fey · 2016 Darrell Mann.

Source: Awards on the live site. The live list stops at 2019.

Past TRIZCONs (archive)

  • TRIZCON2025 — October 6–7, 2025, Riga, Latvia. Hosted and co-organized by the Altshuller Institute and the University of Latvia. Theme: "The Resurgence of TRIZ with AI in support of education, manufacturing and science." A pre-conference workshop on October 3 was offered as a special introduction of the TRIZ methodology to Latvian industry, educators and students. Overview · Agenda · Abstracts & Biographies · Registration
  • TRIZCON2024 — December 14–15, 2024, Beijing, China, hosted at the Sohu Internet Plaza, with a closed, invitation-only pre-conference workshop on December 13. Presentations explored TRIZ applications in industry, education, artificial intelligence, philosophy, and science, for in-person and virtual attendees. Overview and keynote speakers · Abstracts & Bios
  • TRIZCON2023 — Abstracts include "Was Bitcoin creator Satoshi Nakamato a TRIZ practitioner?" (Viesturs Tamužs) and "Enhancing TOP-TRIZ Problem Solving with AI" (Zinovy Royzen). Abstracts.
