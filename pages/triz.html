What is TRIZ

TRIZ is a systematic method for inventive problem-solving, drawn from the study of patents. Genrikh Altshuller showed that strong solutions follow patterns. Those patterns can be taught.

This page will hold: a short definition, who Altshuller was, the 40 principles as a reference — not a dump of every historical essay on the first screen.
