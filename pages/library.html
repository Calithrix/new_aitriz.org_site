Community news

TRIZ news from around the world

Any member of the TRIZ community can post press releases and news to the Institute's news section. The archive runs to forty pages, with regular updates from the "TRIZ Home Page in Japan" edited by Toru Nakagawa, Project Leader of the World TRIZ-related Sites Project (WTSP), alongside announcements from TRIZ associations and providers.

Members also receive the monthly E-Newsletter and News Flashes summarizing current TRIZ happenings: press releases, TRIZ trainings and TRIZ news, sent two to three times a month to over 1,500 subscribers.
