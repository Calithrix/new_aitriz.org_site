Soft Side of TRIZ

A series of TRIZ lessons by Jack Hipple on applying TRIZ in the organizational, business, and the "soft" business/people side of problem solving. "Sometimes we, as TRIZ practitioners, get so involved in advancing algorithms, software, etc. that we forget the very basics of what Altshuller observed and is the basis for everything we do. And that is that there are few really new problems or ways to solve them."

Currie's Corner

Cathie M. Currie, Ph.D. is a cognitive social psychologist who specializes in medical and science education. She is also an Advisor to Altshuller Institute.

  • The Well-Tinkered Mind (April 2011) — Dr. Currie contends that tinkering, a cherished hobby of yesteryear, has reemerged as a novel catalyst for student engagement and higher-level thinking in thousands of school classrooms and after-school programs across our country.
  • TRIZ Takes Case-Based Instruction to a Higher Level (May 2010) — Cathie M. Currie, Ph.D., and Richard Langevin. Policy makers and educators assume that improvement in student learning and instruction requires a lengthy, slow, and detailed process. We need to challenge that assumption.
