TRIZ Certification

TRIZ Certification is one of the cornerstone services offered by the Altshuller Institute. Certification is a verification of the achievement that individuals have attained a level of accomplishment and expertise in the TRIZ world. As one of two international certification organizations in the world, the Altshuller Institute is serious about maintaining a high level of standards and verification. This effort will protect the validity of certification and the integrity of the process.

Certification proves that you have achieved a level of proficiency and understanding of the TRIZ methodology. It is another skill set that you can add to your resume that will help you to distinguish yourself from your peers. Practitioner and Specialist certification single you out as a high level problem solver who can identify the correct problem and deliver solution concepts faster, cheaper and with better results.

There are 3 primary levels of certification as defined by Altshuller Institute: Associate, Practitioner and Specialist. A fourth level of certification will be the TRIZ Master. All AI qualifications are compatible with the ones conferred by the International TRIZ Association (MATRIZ).

Benefits of certification

  • For individuals: confirms mastery of TRIZ concepts, tools and best practices; enables better job performance; enhances career/promotion opportunities; recognizes growth, professional development and competence in TRIZ; increases recognition by peers, employers, and recruiters; sets you apart as an expert and leader in your field.
  • For management: identifies those who have sufficient product development knowledge and experience to move into a leadership position; differentiates between hiring candidates.
  • For organizations: supports an organization's drive for process consistency and discipline in problem solving and innovation. Several leading companies are already sponsoring TRIZ Certification in their corporations, among them Intel, GE, Boeing, Samsung, Hyundai, Corning, Denso, Ericsson, General Dynamics, Shell and DOW.

General provisions

Pre-requisite: Annual or Lifetime membership. Certification of TRIZ was enacted by Genrikh Altshuller at the founding of the Altshuller Institute (AI). The objectives are:

  • To provide a "seal of quality" that will make consumers of various TRIZ services feel confident that they are dealing with a knowledgeable TRIZ person.
  • To help establish definitions of the TRIZ knowledge content, as well as the scope and boundaries of that content.
  • To provide recognition for TRIZ training curricula that complies with the highest standards of contemporary TRIZ education.

The certification is based on objective evaluation of the candidate's knowledge of TRIZ and his/her ability to practically use this knowledge. To be certified, the aspirant has to pass a published standardized process that will demonstrate both his/her comprehensive understanding of the established body of knowledge of TRIZ and the ability to solve real-life problems using tools of TRIZ. The certification is administered by the Certification Board (CB) comprised of leading experts in TRIZ theory and practice (TRIZ Masters all).

Certification levels

TRIZ Associate

Prerequisite: Annual or Lifetime Membership in AI. Applicant should have a good theoretical knowledge of all Examination Topics: Function Analysis; Problem Modeling and Problem Solving (engineering contradictions, the 39 typical parameters, Altshuller's Contradiction Matrix, the 40 Inventive Principles, physical contradictions); and the Laws (Trends/Patterns) of Engineering System Evolution. The test is open-book, multiple-choice; no computers allowed. A test contains 6–8 problems divided into 3 groups. Passing final grade – 75%. Duration of test is up to 4 hours. The fee for annual AI members is $150 USD; if you are not an annual member, you may pay $250 for membership and exam fee. Associate Certification

TRIZ Practitioner

Prerequisites: Annual or Lifetime Member of AI; must have Associate Certification from AI. Examination topics add substance-field (S-F) models and the System of Standard Inventive Solutions, function modeling of problems, and ARIZ (ARIZ-85-C and later versions). Open-book; a test contains 10 problems divided into 5 groups. Passing final grade – 75%; all tests will be graded by 5 members of the TRIZ Certification Board. Duration – up to 8 hours. Fee $400 USD. Practitioner Certification

TRIZ Specialist

The candidate must be a full member of Altshuller Institute (AI) in good standing and hold a certificate of TRIZ Practitioner from AI. He or she must submit verifiable evidence of practical application of TRIZ with at least two years of experience. The applicant should present a certification thesis containing a detailed TRIZ analysis of a real-world technological challenge, submitted to the AI Certification Board at least 8 weeks prior to the thesis defense date. The interview will be conducted by a minimum of 3 AI Certification Board Members. Fee – $800. Specialist Certification

TRIZ Master

TRIZ Master's degree is conferred for research-related contributions to TRIZ development and the practical application of TRIZ, based on a defended dissertation or contributions to practice in the field of TRIZ. To award the TRIZ Master's degree, the Board of Directors of AI will impanel a Qualification Commission (QC) consisting of a number of TRIZ Masters from AI or TRIZ Masters from other TRIZ organizations. University programs

The Altshuller Institute facilitates the training and support of the Bioengineering department at Tulane University, New Orleans, LA. The professors were very excited about the TRIZ training provided and sought practical tools to help the students with their bioengineering projects.

"For the 4th year in a row, Tulane University is offering a TRIZ course as part of the Graduate Engineering Curriculum. This year, 12 students spanning 4 engineering disciplines are learning the basics of TRIZ methods. Some will elect to sit for the certification exam to become TRIZ Associates. Graduates of the program typically become industry leaders in research, design, and manufacturing." — Prof. Khaled Adjerid, Bioengineering Dept. Head

This year's class, 5 students took and passed the official exam and have earned their Associate Certification from the Altshuller Institute (May 2026).
